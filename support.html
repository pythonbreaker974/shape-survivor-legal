Shape Survivor Support

Shape Survivor is an offline-first mobile survival defense game.

Common Questions

Does the game need internet?
No. Gameplay and progress are stored locally on your device.

Why did my progress disappear?
Progress can be removed if the app is deleted, browser storage is cleared, or the device is reset.

Does the game collect personal data?
No. See the Privacy Policy for details.
